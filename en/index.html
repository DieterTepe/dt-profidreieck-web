Item 04 · Welding technology

DT-ProfiSchweissnaht:
a calculation that shows how it calculates

Calculates welds in structural steelwork to EN 1993-1-8 and in classical mechanical engineering – showing every single step on the way: from the weld layout via centroid, second moments of area and principal axes to the equivalent stress at the governing point. No result without a calculation path, no figure without a source.

1:27 minutes: tube 114.3 × 6 under torsion – input, weld layout, calculation path with self-check.

◫

Weld layout instead of weld length

The real weld geometry arises from the profile and the edges actually welded: segments, centroid, second moments of area, principal axes, section moduli – and from these the governing point. The weld layout is drawn, with centroid cross and axes.

∑

The calculation path is the product

More than thirty steps, each with formula, inserted figures, result and source – standard, textbook or expressly marked as a practical guide value.

✓

It checks itself – and says so

Arithmetic checks run with every calculation: are the static moments zero? Does I_p really equal I_y + I_z? A missing check mark means an arithmetic error in the program – reported separately from a check that is not satisfied.

!

It states what it does not check

Every output carries a list of the points this program deliberately does not check – base material, buckling and stability, component stiffness, execution and more. In the result itself, not in the small print.

EN

Four design worlds

Structural steelwork to EN 1993-1-8, stainless steels to EN 1993-1-4, aluminium to EN 1999-1-1 and classical mechanical engineering (Roloff/Matek, Decker) – kept apart, never mixed. Either verification or sizing of the throat thickness.

⤓

More than the weld

Heat control and preheating to EN 1011-2, cost, time and wire consumption as well as drawing symbols to EN ISO 2553. Outputs: print/PDF, Word (.rtf) including the weld layout and a dedicated file format (.dts) that stores the inputs rather than the results.

The trial version calculates everything – with the complete calculation path on screen. Only the outputs are locked.

Frequently asked questions

Answered briefly

Does it really run completely offline?

Yes. Each program is a single HTML file that runs in the browser – on phone, tablet and PC. After the download no internet connection is needed, there is no account and no installation.

How does the purchase work?

The purchase button takes you to the Digistore24 order page (common payment methods, invoice included). The full version is ready to download immediately after payment.

What is the difference between trial and full version?

With DT-ProfiDreieck and DT-ProfiSchraube the free version shows how the program works with a limited range of functions; the full version unlocks all calculation cases, assistants and exports. With DT-ProfiPassung and DT-ProfiSchweissnaht the calculation is already complete – there only the outputs are locked: save, open, print and Word.

Is any data transmitted or stored?

No. The programs calculate locally in the browser. This website uses neither cookies nor tracking; the details are in the privacy policy (in German).

Do the programs replace the qualified engineer?

No, and they do not try to. They calculate transparently and name their limits. Professional responsibility for load assumptions, material selection, execution and approval stays with the user; every result has to be checked against the original standards and your own approval.

Which sets of rules are used?

DT-ProfiSchraube to VDI 2230 Part 1, DT-ProfiPassung to ISO 286, DIN 7190 and ISO 2768, DT-ProfiSchweissnaht to EN 1993-1-8, EN 1993-1-4, EN 1999-1-1, EN 1011-2 and EN ISO 2553 as well as Roloff/Matek and Decker in mechanical engineering. Every calculation step names its source.

Can I override table values?

Yes. In DT-ProfiSchweissnaht every preset value can be replaced by your own via a check box. The program visibly distinguishes between a table value from the standard, a practical guide value and a price assumption with its year.

Who do I contact with questions?

Just send an e-mail to Dieter.Tepe@live.de – the developer answers personally. Feedback from practice flows into the updates.
